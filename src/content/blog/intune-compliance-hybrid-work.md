Microsoft's latest compliance policy update tightens how conditional access
interacts with device health signals — relevant for any tenant running a
hybrid fleet of managed and lightly-managed devices.

## The practical impact

For EMM administrators, the main change is in how quickly a device falls
out of compliance after a failed health check, and what that means for
access to line-of-business apps in the interim.
