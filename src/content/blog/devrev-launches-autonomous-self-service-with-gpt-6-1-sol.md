DevRev has launched its new autonomous self-service solution powered by OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol. This model is designed to enhance enterprise service by balancing reasoning, speed, and cost, enabling operations teams to handle complex customer and employee requests effectively. The solution is available through API integration, allowing businesses to implement it within their existing infrastructure. According to Ahmed Bashir, DevRev's CTO, the model excels in reasoning and reliability while maintaining efficient latency, making it a cost-effective choice for enterprises. DevRev's Agent Studio allows users to define tool behaviors and permissions, ensuring actions are traceable and reversible, which Ahmed claims strikes a perfect balance in handling nuanced requests quickly and sustainably.
