Epoch AI has reported that **gpt-6-1-sol** has achieved a perfect score of 100.0% on the FrontierMath benchmark, establishing itself as the new leader in this category. This is a significant milestone for the model, which has also entered the top five on both the ARC-AGI-2 and GPQA Diamond benchmarks, showcasing its versatile capabilities across different evaluation metrics.

Additionally, **claude-opus-5-5** and **claude-sonnet-5-5** have both secured positions in the top five on the ARC-AGI-2 and GPQA Diamond benchmarks, respectively. The latter also made its mark by entering the top five on Humanity's Last Exam, indicating a strong performance across multiple challenging tasks.

This round of evaluations also marks the first tracked measurements for several models, including **claude-haiku-5-5**, **gpt-6-1-sol**, **claude-sonnet-5-5**, **gpt-6-luna**, **gpt-6-sol**, **claude-opus-5-5**, and **muse-spark-1-3**. These initial measurements provide a baseline for future comparisons and highlight the growing diversity and capability of AI models in the industry.
