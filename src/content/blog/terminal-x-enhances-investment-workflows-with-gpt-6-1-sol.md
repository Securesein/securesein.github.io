Terminal X has released GPT-6.1 Sol, an advanced AI model designed to enhance investment workflows by improving judgment accuracy and cost efficiency. Compared to its predecessor, GPT-6.1 Sol offers a 25% higher detection rate for recommendations that deviate from a fund's mandate and reduces query costs by approximately 30%. This model is available via API and can be deployed on-premise or in private clouds. According to Terminal X, GPT-6.1 Sol's long-context reasoning capabilities and token efficiency enable deeper evaluation pipelines, resulting in a 220 basis point improvement in agreement with human reviewers over the next-best model. Terminal X reports that these enhancements have led to significant savings, with one customer achieving $2.4 million in annualized savings on due diligence and investment workflows.
